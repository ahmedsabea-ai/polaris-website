Supply chain management

How a product gets from supplier to customer, and where the cost and delay hide along the way. For planners, buyers, warehouse and logistics staff, and the managers who run them. The course follows the body of knowledge behind the ASCM (APICS) CSCP exam.

  • Supply chain design: strategy, network and technology
  • Planning and execution: procuring and delivering goods and services
  • Working with supply chain partners, including returns and recalls
  • Improvement: measuring performance, managing risk, meeting standards

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt

A structured way to find why a process wastes time or produces defects, then fix it and keep it fixed. Participants work through the DMAIC cycle with hands-on workshops on the statistics.

  • Define: project selection, process mapping, voice of the customer
  • Measure: value stream mapping, data collection, process capability
  • Analyse: analysis tools, regression and ANOVA
  • Improve: Kaizen, Poka Yoke, 5S, line balancing, Kanban, Just in Time
  • Control: process and lean controls, project closure, exam preparation

For engineers, quality and process managers, production and customer service managers, and consultants who want to lead improvement projects.

A project you can afford to get wrong

The Challenge of Egypt puts a team in charge of building a pyramid. The setting is ancient, the problems are the ones every project has: unclear scope, limited resources, risks nobody planned for, and a client who changes his mind.

It works for project teams, for departments that depend on each other, and for managers who want their people to feel what good planning is worth before the next real project starts.

Description to check against the version of the simulation you deliver.

Ask about a session for your team
  1. BriefThe team receives the assignment, the roles and the constraints.
  2. PlanThey agree scope, schedule, resources and who decides what.
  3. BuildThe project runs in rounds. Events hit the plan and the team has to respond.
  4. ReviewAfter each round the team looks at what happened and changes how it works.
  5. Take it back to workThe day ends with the specific things the team will do differently on its own projects.
